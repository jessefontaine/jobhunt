Actions

fetch → score → digest

Your normal daily run, and the one to press if you are not sure which to use — it does the other three in order and skips work already done.

only pull new listings into the store

When you just want to see what is out there without spending Claude calls, or a single source failed and you are retrying that one.

score unscored listings with Claude

When listings are sitting unscored, or after editing your profile, CV or preferences — tick rescore to replace the scores those edits made stale.

re-render digests/<today>.md and shortlist.md

When you rated in the browser and want the markdown files to match, or you want today's digest rebuilt after scores changed. Nothing is fetched or scored.

store a vacancy you found yourself, and score it

When you (or Claude) come across a role the sources did not turn up: paste the link and it joins the queue, the digest and the ratings loop like any other. If the site blocks the fetch, fill in the title and employer by hand.
